# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful, assume good intent while discussing ideas, accept constructive feedback, and focus criticism on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, sexualized attention, publishing another person's private information, or conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainers' GitHub profiles. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical, with attention to the safety of everyone involved.

Maintainers are responsible for applying this policy fairly and may recuse themselves when they have a conflict of interest.
